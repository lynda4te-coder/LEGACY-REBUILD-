Program Curriculum

LEGACY REBUILD

A 15-Week Journey of Transformation, Leadership, and Impact

Program Overview

This 30+ page curriculum is designed to rebuild the internal and external structures of an individual's life. It follows 5 integrated pillars across a 12-15 week structure involving videos, workbooks, reflection, and real-world application.

PILLAR 1: INNER RESET (Weeks 1-4)

Addressing the root of stagnation by healing the internal life.

Week 1: Emotional Awareness & Discovery

Theme: Facing What Has Been Hidden

Learning Focus: Developing self-awareness—the foundation of all transformation.

  • Understanding Emotional Wounds (Rejection, Abandonment, Betrayal, Shame)
  • Emotional Triggers & Patterns: Connecting reactions to memories
  • Guided Practice: Emotional Mapping & Trigger Tracker
Assignment: Reflection Essay on shaping experiences and recognized patterns.

Week 2: Healing & Inner Freedom

Theme: Releasing the Weight of the Past

  • The Cost of Unhealed Pain in Relationships and Opportunities
  • The 5-Step Healing Process: Acknowledge, Accept, Release, Reframe, Rebuild
  • The Power of Forgiveness: A decision for personal freedom
Assignment: Forgiveness Letter (private) and Release Ritual.

Week 3: Identity Restoration & Self-Worth

Theme: Rebuilding Who You Are

  • Identity vs. Labels: Moving from past failures to truth-based purpose
  • The Strength Mirror: Identifying values, strengths, and passions
  • Assignment: Create a Personal Identity Statement.

Week 4: Mental Renewal & Mindset Shift

Theme: Thinking Your Way Into a New Life

  • The Thought Cycle: Thoughts → Beliefs → Actions → Results
  • Rewiring the Mind: Thought Replacement, Visualization, and Affirmations
  • Growth vs. Fixed Mindsets

PILLAR 2: PURPOSE & LIFE DESIGN (Weeks 5-7)

Week 5: Purpose Discovery & Clarity

Focus: Aligning gifts, passion, pain, and impact to define "Why I am here."

Week 6: Vision Crafting & Goal Setting

Focus: Designing the 5-year ideal life using SMART Goals and purposeful alignment.

Week 7: Life Systems & Productivity Mastery

Focus: Systems vs. Goals. Designing morning/evening routines and habit trackers.

PILLAR 3: WEALTH MASTERY (Weeks 8-11)

Week 8-11: Financial Intelligence

  • Week 8: Money Mindset & Financial Audits
  • Week 9: Income Growth (Value creation & multiple streams)
  • Week 10: Wealth Building (Earn → Save → Invest → Multiply)
  • Week 11: Legacy Planning (Generational wealth & stewardship)

PILLAR 4: FAMILY & RELATIONSHIPS (Weeks 12-13)

Week 12: Communication & EQ

Learning self-regulation, empathy, and active listening to resolve conflict.

Week 13: Parenting & Partnerships

Breaking generational patterns and building intentional family values.

PILLAR 5: LEADERSHIP & LEGACY (Weeks 14-15)

Week 14: Leadership Identity

Self-leadership, strategic thinking, and intentional decision-making.

Week 15: Legacy Building & Mentorship

Living beyond yourself through multiplication and community impact.

Certification & Assessment

To be certified as a Legacy Leader, participants must maintain 80% attendance and complete the following:

Component Weight Deliverables
Continuous Assessment 30% Weekly assignments and participation
Practical Application 30% Pillar-specific growth plans
Final Portfolio 25% Compiled Legacy Transformation Portfolio
Final Presentation 15% "Legacy Defense" (10-15 minute presentation)

Grading System

  • 85-100%: Distinction
  • 70-84%: Merit
  • 60-69%: Pass
